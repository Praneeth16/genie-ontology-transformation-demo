# Delivery Health

Domain: Enterprise Transformation

Subdomain: Delivery and Risk

Owner: Transformation Delivery Director

Synonyms: RAG, traffic light, initiative status, program health

Description: The current Green, Amber, or Red assessment for a transformation initiative.

## Definition

Green means the initiative is on plan and the team can manage its open issues. Amber means the initiative needs active management but does not yet require executive intervention. Red means the initiative needs executive attention because delivery, value, risk, or a decision is outside the team's control.

Health is a management assessment. It should be read with schedule, milestones, risks, forecast value, and the latest steering evidence.

## Calculation

The governed counts are `Red Initiatives` and `Amber Initiatives` in `mv_delivery_health`. The initiative level assessment is stored in `v_initiative_current`.
