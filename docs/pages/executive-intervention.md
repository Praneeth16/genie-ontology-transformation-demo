# Executive Intervention

Domain: Enterprise Transformation

Subdomain: Delivery and Risk

Owner: Chief Transformation Officer

Synonyms: leadership action, escalation, steering decision, committee intervention

Description: A decision or action that the executive committee must take because the initiative team lacks the authority or resources to resolve it.

## Definition

An Executive Intervention has a named issue, an accountable owner, a decision or action, and a due date. The Transformation Value Office raises an intervention when an initiative is red, a critical risk is overdue, a milestone is overdue, or a material forecast shortfall needs leadership action.

The intervention list should include the evidence behind the status. It should not treat a risk or delay as proof that value has already been lost.
