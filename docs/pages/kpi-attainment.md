# KPI Attainment

Domain: Enterprise Transformation

Subdomain: Operating Performance

Owner: Enterprise Performance Director

Synonyms: target attainment, operating target achievement, KPI performance

Description: A direction aware comparison of an operating KPI actual value with its target.

## Definition

KPI Attainment compares one operating KPI with its target for one business unit and reporting month. It must not combine KPIs with different units.

For a KPI where higher is better, attainment is actual divided by target. For a KPI where lower is better, attainment is target divided by actual. A value above one means the KPI is better than target.

The governed measure is `Attainment` in `mv_operating_performance`.
